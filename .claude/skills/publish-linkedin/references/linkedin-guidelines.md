# LinkedIn Publication Guidelines

Strategic guidelines for creating high-engagement LinkedIn carousel posts.

## Carousel Structure Blueprint

### Slide 1 - Cover (The Hook)

**Purpose**: Stop the scroll, promise clear value

**Elements**:
- Bold, provocative title (5-8 words max)
- Clear value promise or curiosity gap
- Minimal text, maximum impact
- No clutter, no logos

**Examples**:
- "7 AI Tools That 10x My Productivity"
- "The Framework That Changed How I Lead"
- "Why Most Product Roadmaps Fail"

### Slide 2 - Context (The Why)

**Purpose**: Establish relevance and stakes

**Elements**:
- Problem statement or observation
- Personal credibility hook (if relevant)
- Why this matters NOW
- Sets up the transformation

**Examples**:
- "After building 4 startups, I noticed a pattern..."
- "Most teams waste 40% of their roadmap on features nobody wants."
- "The education system wasn't built for students who learn differently."

### Slides 3 to N-2 - Content (The Meat)

**Purpose**: Deliver the core value

**Rules**:
- ONE idea per slide
- Numbered or structured for easy following
- Actionable, specific, concrete
- Visual hierarchy: headline + 1-3 supporting points
- Use icons, diagrams, or bullets sparingly

**Content Types**:
- Steps in a process (numbered)
- Tips/tactics (bulleted)
- Framework components (visual diagram)
- Before/after comparisons
- Mistake + fix pairs

### Slide N-1 - Summary (The Screenshot)

**Purpose**: Provide shareable recap

**Elements**:
- Condensed checklist of all key points
- Designed to be screenshot-worthy
- High information density
- Clear visual structure

**Examples**:
- Quick reference checklist
- Framework diagram
- TL;DR summary with bullets

### Slide N - CTA (The Close)

**Purpose**: Single focused action

**Elements**:
- ONE clear ask (not multiple)
- Relevant to content just consumed
- Easy to execute
- Creates engagement signal

**CTA Options** (pick ONE):
- "Save this for later" (bookmark prompt)
- "Follow for more [topic]" (follower growth)
- "Comment your experience with [X]" (engagement)
- "Share with someone who needs this" (reach)
- "DM me '[keyword]' for [resource]" (lead gen)

## Caption Structure

### Hook (First 2 lines - visible before "See more")

- Pattern interrupt or curiosity gap
- Personal stake or bold claim
- Must compel the click

**Examples**:
- "I wasted 2 years building the wrong product. Here's what I learned:"
- "This framework saved my company from bankruptcy:"
- "Stop doing [common mistake]. Do this instead:"

### Teaser (After "See more")

- 2-3 sentences expanding on the hook
- Preview what's in the carousel
- Build anticipation without giving everything away

### CTA (End of caption)

- Mirror the carousel's final slide CTA
- Add urgency or social proof if natural
- Keep it simple

### Hashtags (3-5 total)

**Mix**:
- 1-2 broad reach hashtags (500K+ followers)
- 2-3 niche/specific hashtags (10K-100K followers)

**Topics to consider**:
- Industry: #edtech #healthtech #startups
- Role: #productmanagement #engineering #leadership
- Topic: #ai #machinelearning #mtss #specialeducation
- Meta: #linkedintips #careeradvice #productivity

## Visual Design Principles

### Typography

- **Title**: 48-60pt equivalent, bold
- **Body**: 24-32pt equivalent, readable on mobile
- **Minimal text**: 30-40 words max per slide
- **High contrast**: Black on white or vice versa

### Layout

- **Margins**: Generous (100px+ equivalent)
- **Alignment**: Center or left-aligned (consistent)
- **Hierarchy**: Clear visual levels
- **Breathing room**: Don't crowd

### Brand Consistency

- Same color palette across all slides
- Consistent typography treatment
- Recurring visual elements (icons, dividers)
- Professional, not flashy

## Engagement Optimization

### Best Posting Times (US)

- Tuesday-Thursday: 8-10am EST
- Wednesday: 12-1pm EST
- Avoid weekends and Monday mornings

### First Hour Strategy

- Engage with comments immediately
- Reply with value-add, not just "thanks"
- Ask follow-up questions
- Build conversation threads

### Post-Publish Actions

1. Share to LinkedIn story with teaser
2. Cross-post to relevant LinkedIn groups
3. Tag relevant connections (sparingly, with value)
4. Repurpose to Twitter/X thread

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing:

- [ ] Hook makes someone stop scrolling
- [ ] Each slide has ONE clear point
- [ ] Text is readable on mobile (not too small)
- [ ] Visual style is consistent across all slides
- [ ] Summary slide is screenshot-worthy
- [ ] CTA is single and clear
- [ ] Caption hook visible before "See more"
- [ ] 3-5 relevant hashtags included
- [ ] Proofread for typos
- [ ] PDF opens correctly
